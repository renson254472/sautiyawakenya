Who can work with Sautiyawakenya?

Individuals, businesses, leaders and organisations seeking communications support. We discuss fit, scope and deliverables before engagement.

Can you guarantee positive media coverage?

No. Editorial decisions belong to journalists and publishers. We develop credible stories and pursue opportunities. Any agreed paid placements should be clearly identified.

Can you remove negative content online?

Removal cannot be guaranteed. We can assess issues, prepare factual corrections and develop a responsible response. Legal complaints may require a qualified lawyer.

What if I am facing an active crisis?

Email with Urgent communications enquiry in the subject and a brief, non-sensitive outline. Confirm availability before relying on a response. This is not an emergency service and response times are not guaranteed.

How does pricing work?

Scope, deliverables, timelines and fees are agreed after an initial discussion. This site does not take payments or create subscriptions.
